Portfolio

Buy USDC with card — MoonPay is live in Portfolio

4 September 2026 · Black DEX desk

You can now buy USDC with a card from the Black DEX Portfolio page. The flow uses Privy’s fund wallet rail. When card onramps are enabled, MoonPay (and other providers Privy surfaces) settles Base USDC into the same connected wallet you already use on the book.

Black DEX does not take custody of the purchase. The card issuer and MoonPay run KYC and payment. The venue only receives the USDC that lands in your wallet, then you deposit it as margin if you want to trade.

01

Connect

Open black-dex.online and connect with wallet, Google, X or email.

02

Open Portfolio

Go to Portfolio. The Buy USDC control sits on the value card.

03

Complete MoonPay

Choose amount. Finish the card / MoonPay screen. USDC arrives on Base.

04

Deposit to trade

Move USDC onto the Orderly account when you are ready to trade perps.

Earn

Morpho Earn vaults are on /earn

4 September 2026 · Black DEX desk

Earn on Black DEX now points at Morpho vaults routed through the same connected wallet as the terminal. Deposits and withdrawals are ERC-4626 transactions from that wallet. Yield is market-driven. It is not a locked or guaranteed APY.

S

Steakhouse Prime USDC

Base. USDC vault. Same wallet you use to trade. Wrapper fee applies on the Privy Earn path.

P

Sentora pathUSD

Tempo. pathUSD vault. Deposit and withdraw on-chain from the connected Black DEX wallet.

Use Earn when you want idle USDC or pathUSD to sit in a Morpho strategy instead of sitting in the wallet. Use Portfolio deposit when you want that capital on the Orderly book. The two ledgers are separate.

Open Earn Open Portfolio

Vault APYs move with utilization and curator policy. Black DEX does not guarantee return. Read the vault page before depositing.
