Product notes from the Black DEX desk. Not a social feed. Two live items today: card on-ramp for Base USDC, and Earn vaults wrapped through Privy on Morpho and Tempo.

4 Sep 2026 · Portfolio

Buy USDC with MoonPay on Portfolio

Card checkout now sits on the Portfolio overview. Connect the Black DEX wallet, tap Buy USDC, and Privy opens MoonPay first, with Stripe and Coinbase as fallbacks.

Destination is Base USDC on the connected address — the same EVM wallet you use to trade. This is not Orderly ledger USDC and not Tempo pathUSD.

Payment rails are debit/credit, Apple Pay and Google Pay where the provider allows them. Debit cards usually clear more often than credit. Settlement is the provider’s clock, not instant fill on the book.

The flow only appears after Card onramps are enabled on the Privy app and https://black-dex.online is on the allowed-origins list.

Open black-dex.online/portfolio · Checkout branded MoonPay / Stripe / Coinbase via Privy Funding.

Morpho Earn vaults on Black DEX

/earn now lists Privy-wrapped vaults instead of Orderly strategy cards. Deposits use the connected Black DEX wallet. Yield is variable. A performance fee is taken from yield only.

Steakhouse Prime USDC

Base. Asset USDC. Morpho strategy behind a Privy fee wrapper. Black DEX keeps 10% of generated yield. Principal can be withdrawn to the same wallet.

Sentora pathUSD

Tempo (chain 4217). Asset pathUSD. Same 10% yield cut. You need pathUSD on Tempo in that wallet. Tempo fees are paid in the stable, not ETH.

APY on the cards is not a guaranteed rate. Public Morpho vaults without the wrapper do not pay Black DEX a fee.
