Infrastructure requirements

Permissionless perpetual launches on Orderly have their own eligibility rules.

Eligible permissionless markets require a $25,000 USDT Insurance Fund deposit under the Orderly listing framework. This is not an Occult Black listing fee.

Projects remain responsible for liquidity and price-feed requirements. Treatment of the deposit follows Orderly rules.

Questions

Is this a guaranteed listing?

No. Markets go live only after applicable Orderly and venue requirements are met.

Does Occult Black provide the $25,000 Insurance Fund?

No. That deposit is an Orderly infrastructure requirement, separate from service fees.

Does Black DEX guarantee volume?

No. Volume depends on liquidity, trader demand and market conditions.

Who operates Black DEX?

Black DEX is the builder frontend. Matching and settlement run on Orderly Network. Occult Black coordinates market launches.
