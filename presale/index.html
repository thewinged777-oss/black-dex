07 / Questions

Before you
make a decision.

Is the presale open?

No. This page is an advance information page. The presale is not accepting contributions, and there is no live purchase contract or payment address. Do not send funds to any address claiming to be the Black DEX presale wallet. Follow Black DEX for an official opening announcement.

Which network and payment asset are planned?

The proposed network is Solana mainnet. The planned payment asset is Circle's native USDC SPL token, not a bridged or look-alike asset. The official USDC mint is shown in the payment information above. The $BLACK token mint and treasury receiving address have not been published.

Do I need to connect my wallet?

No wallet connection is planned for the initial contribution flow. If the sale opens, buyers will send USDC themselves to the verified treasury address and submit the transaction signature and sending wallet address for verification. The website will never ask for a seed phrase or private key. Any public allocation record should use only the transaction and wallet data needed to prove the allocation, not personal identity details.

How will the treasury be secured?

The planned treasury setup is a dedicated hardware-backed Solana multisig, with multiple independent signers and a published approval threshold. The final multisig address and signer policy will be published only after setup and independent verification.

How does presale vesting work?

The proposed schedule is 10% at token generation and the remaining 90% released linearly over nine months. Final terms will be set out in the sale documentation and enforced by the published vesting contract.

Does the presale price guarantee the market price?

No. The $0.01 figure is the proposed presale price and implies a $10 million fully diluted valuation. It does not guarantee a listing price, liquidity, returns or future market value.

What will $BLACK be used for?

The planned utility roadmap includes ecosystem incentives, trading-related benefits, community programmes and selected governance participation. Features will only be described as active once implemented and documented.
