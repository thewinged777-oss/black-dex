The current proposal is to allocate 15% of eligible net profits generated by Black DEX trading fees and market-making activities to a token-holder rewards programme.

This is a proposed economic model, not a guaranteed return, fixed yield or promise of regular payments. 07 / Questions

Before you
make a decision.

Is the presale open?

Yes. Contributions are accepted by manual transfer of native USDC on Solana to the receiving wallet shown on this page. A transfer is not an allocation confirmation until the project verifies the transaction and records it. Always check the address and network before sending.

Which network and payment asset are planned?

The sale network is Solana mainnet. The planned payment asset is Circle's native USDC SPL token, not a bridged or look-alike asset. The official USDC mint is shown in the payment information above. The $BLACK token mint and treasury receiving address have not been published.

Do I need to connect my wallet?

No wallet connection is planned for the initial contribution flow. If the sale opens, buyers will send USDC themselves to the verified treasury address and submit the transaction signature and sending wallet address for verification. The website will never ask for a seed phrase or private key. Any public allocation record should use only the transaction and wallet data needed to prove the allocation, not personal identity details.

Will $BLACK holders receive a share of Black DEX profits?

The current proposal is to allocate 15% of eligible net profits generated by Black DEX trading fees and market-making activities to a token-holder rewards programme. This is not a guaranteed return. The definition of eligible net profit, legal structure, eligibility, calculation and distribution rules are not finalized. Because profit participation may cause the token to qualify as a financial instrument or security, the proposal is subject to specialist legal review and may need to be restructured.

How will early presale pricing work?

The proposed model uses multiple public-sale tiers, with earlier tiers priced below later tiers. Each tier will have a clearly disclosed price, token allocation and applicable limits. Exact tier prices, allocations and opening conditions have not yet been finalized. No tier is currently open and no contribution should be sent.

How will the treasury be secured?

The planned treasury setup is a dedicated hardware-backed Solana multisig, with multiple independent signers and a published approval threshold. The final multisig address and signer policy will be published only after setup and independent verification.

How long will the presale run?

The presale window is 90 calendar days, starting 31 October 2026 and scheduled to end 29 January 2027. It may close earlier if the hard cap is reached or all available tokens are sold.

How do I claim my tokens before exchange listings?

After TGE and before the first exchange listing, the planned claim process will let you return to the official Black DEX presale claim page and connect the same Solana wallet that sent your original USDC contribution. The system will match that wallet to the verified presale contribution record. You will then be able to claim or withdraw the amount that is unlocked and vested at that time. Under the proposed vesting schedule, 10% is unlocked at TGE; the remaining 90% becomes claimable linearly over nine months. Connecting a different wallet will not establish ownership of the allocation. Never share your seed phrase or private key. This claim feature is not live yet and will only be enabled after the token mint, allocation records, claim/vesting contracts and security review are complete.

Can I withdraw all presale tokens immediately after TGE?

No. The proposed schedule unlocks 10% at TGE, with the remaining 90% released linearly over nine months. Before listings, you may claim only the portion already unlocked and vested. The exact claim start time and contract mechanics will be published before TGE.

Does the presale price guarantee the market price?

No. The $0.01 figure is the proposed presale price and implies a $10 million fully diluted valuation. It does not guarantee a listing price, liquidity, returns or future market value.

What will $BLACK be used for?

The planned utility roadmap includes ecosystem incentives, trading-related benefits, community programmes and selected governance participation. Features will only be described as active once implemented and documented.
